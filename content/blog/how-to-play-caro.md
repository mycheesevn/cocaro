## The Goal

Connect **five of your marks in a row** — horizontally, vertically, or diagonally — before your opponent does. You play **X**, the computer plays **O**, and X always moves first.

## The Board

Gomoku is played on the intersections of a grid, like a page of Vietnamese school notebook paper (giấy ô ly). In this app you can choose three board sizes:

- **10×10** — quick games, great for learning
- **15×15** — the classic size (default)
- **20×20** — long, strategic battles

On each turn you place **one mark** on any empty intersection. There are no captures and no movement — a mark stays where it was placed for the whole game.

## The Vietnamese Rules — Two Twists

This app plays the rules used across Vietnam, which differ from Japanese renju:

- **Exactly five blocked at BOTH ends does NOT win.** If your line of exactly five has an opponent's mark (or the edge of the board) immediately at both ends, it is a "dead five" and the game continues. One open end is enough — that five wins.
- **Six or more ALWAYS wins.** An "overline" of 6, 7, or more connected marks wins immediately, even if both ends are blocked.
- **No forbidden moves.** Unlike renju, there are no banned double-threes or double-fours — every placement is legal.
- **Draw:** if the board fills completely with no winning line, the game is a draw.

## Basic Strategy

- **Take the center.** The center intersection touches the most possible lines — the strongest first move.
- **Open threes are threats.** Three in a row with both ends empty ("open three") becomes an open four next move, and an open four cannot be stopped: block one end and your opponent plays the other.
- **Block fours immediately.** When the opponent has four in a row, count the open ends. One open end: you must play there now. Two open ends: only winning first can save you.
- **The double-three (3–3) is the classic killer.** One move that creates two open threes at once can only be half-blocked — the other three grows into an open four. Look for crossing cells where a row and a column both need the same intersection.
- **Mind the blocked-ends rule.** Before celebrating a five, check both ends. And when defending, remember a five can be killed in advance by sealing its far end early.
